04 GOOD TO KNOW

A few little
answers.

What is Tanukio?+

Tanukio is an Android app for tracking expenses, scanning receipts, following budgets and organizing purchase warranties.

Do I need to create an account?+

No. Tanukio does not require a Tanukio account.

Does Tanukio scan receipts?+

Yes. Tanukio can scan receipts and suggest details such as the merchant, date and amount. You can check the information before you save it.

Can I back up my data?+

Yes. You can create a backup of your Tanukio data and restore it when needed.
